Expense Tracker 💰📊

Expense Tracker is a user-friendly finance management application that helps users monitor their income and expenses effectively. The app allows users to log financial transactions, categorize them, and visualize their spending habits through interactive bar and pie charts. 📈📉

Key Features ✨:
•	Income & Expense Logging 📝: Easily add and manage records of income and expenses.
•	Data Visualization 📊: Generate dynamic bar and pie charts for clear insights into financial trends.
•	Export Options 📤: Export financial reports as PDF or Excel files for offline access and record-keeping.
•	Budget Awareness 💡: Encourages mindful spending by providing users with detailed overviews of their financial activities.

This tool is perfect for individuals looking to improve their financial health through better tracking and reporting. 💪💸
